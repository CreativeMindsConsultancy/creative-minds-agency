# Creative Minds Consultancy

**AI-first digital agency for Irish SMEs — Dublin, Ireland**

---

## About the Agency

Creative Minds Consultancy is a Dublin-based digital agency founded by Kimberley, built around a single conviction: that small and medium enterprises deserve access to the same cutting-edge AI tools that large enterprises use — without the enterprise price tag or complexity.

We work closely with Irish SMEs to design, build, and automate the digital infrastructure they need to compete in a modern market. Every engagement is hands-on, strategy-led, and built to deliver measurable outcomes.

- AI tool selection, onboarding, and training
