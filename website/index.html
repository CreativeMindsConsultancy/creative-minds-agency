About the Founder

Built from
the inside out

I'm Kimberley, founder of Creative Minds Consultancy. Based in Dublin, I built this agency after years working as an Executive Assistant at the highest levels of corporate Ireland — giving me a rare, first-hand understanding of what ambitious businesses actually need to succeed.

What began as deep curiosity evolved into a mission. I taught myself web design, WordPress, SEO, and ultimately AI — building real, transferable skills through real-world projects. When AI tools began transforming how work gets done, I was already there, experimenting and applying.

Today, Creative Minds Consultancy helps Irish SMEs access the same quality of digital presence and AI capability that was once reserved for large corporations — without the enterprise price tag or complexity.
